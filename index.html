About PIXEL

Design beyond the screen.

Good design is more than making things look attractive. It communicates an idea, builds an identity people remember, and makes a digital product feel natural to use.

That is why we don't split branding, interface design and development into separate jobs. Strategy shapes the brand, the brand shapes the interface, and the interface becomes the website. One thread, from the first idea to launch day.

We work with startups, entrepreneurs, small businesses and creators, always in the open and always alongside you.

What we do

Three services, one process.

Each one is strong alone. Together they keep your idea consistent from the first sketch to the finished site.

01

Brand Identity & Strategy

Before anything is drawn, we work out who you are and why it matters. Then we build an identity that looks and sounds like you, everywhere it appears.

  • Brand discovery and creative direction
  • Logo and visual identity systems
  • Color palettes and typography
  • Brand guidelines
  • Social media visual assets
02

UI/UX Design

Interfaces that are lovely to look at and effortless to use, designed around real people and the real things they came to do.

  • Website and mobile app interface design
  • User flows and wireframes
  • Interactive prototypes
  • Design systems and components
  • Usability improvements
03

Web Development

We turn the design into fast, responsive pages that behave exactly the way they were drawn, on every screen.

  • Landing pages and business websites
  • Responsive front-end development
  • Interactive elements and CSS animation
  • Performance and usability tuning
  • Cross-browser testing and maintenance
